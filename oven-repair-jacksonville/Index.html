Diagnostic Scope

Range & Cooktop Issues We Repair

Oven and range repairs require careful verification of power input, safety igniter resistance, and temperature sensor calibration. We isolate mechanical, electrical, and gas component failures safely.

ISSUE 01

Oven Not Heating / Baking

Diagnosing burnt bake elements, weak glow-bar igniters, thermal switches, and relay control board failures.

ISSUE 02

Gas Burner Clicks / Won't Light

Cleaning clogged burner ports, replacing spark igniter electrodes, spark modules, and safety valves.

ISSUE 03

Uneven Temperature / Burning

Testing RTD oven temperature sensors, thermostat calibration, and convection fan assemblies.

ISSUE 04

Electric Surface Element Failures

Replacing burnt radiant cooktop elements, dual-ring infinite switches, and glass top connections.

ISSUE 05

Door & Latch Lockout Errors

Resolving self-clean motor latch lockouts, broken door hinges, and door seal heat leaks.

ISSUE 06

Control Panel & Display Errors

Diagnosing unresponsive touchpads, control board display error codes (F-codes), and power relays.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jacksonville Oven & Range Repair FAQ

Why is my gas oven taking 20+ minutes to preheat?

This is usually caused by a weakening igniter glow bar. As the igniter wears down, it takes longer to draw sufficient amperage to trigger the gas valve.

Why are my gas stovetop burners clicking continuously?

Continuous clicking usually happens when moisture, food residue, or grease gets inside the spark igniter switches or electrode points.

Can a cracked glass cooktop surface be repaired?

Cracked ceramic glass cooktops require replacing the glass top assembly for safety reasons. We can inspect the internal burners to verify if replacement glass is cost-effective.
