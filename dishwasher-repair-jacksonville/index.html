Targeted Mechanical & Electrical Troubleshooting

A dishwasher that won't drain might suffer from a burnt drain pump motor coil, or it could simply be restricted by food debris behind the check valve. Likewise, improper wash pressure can stem from a clogged chopper blade or a failing wash motor capacitor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jacksonville Dishwasher Repair FAQ

Why is there standing water in the bottom of my dishwasher?

Standing water typically points to a clogged drain filter/chopper blade, a blocked drain hose loop under your sink, a stuck check valve, or a failed drain pump motor.

Why are my dishes coming out gritty or wet?

Gritty dishes usually indicate blocked spray arm nozzles or a failing circulation pump. Wet dishes after a heated dry cycle usually signal a burnt heating element or open thermal fuse.

Why does my dishwasher keep blinking and won't start?

Blinking lights or error beeps usually point to a door latch microswitch failure (preventing the unit from confirming it is locked) or an active leak sensor detecting water in the drain pan.
