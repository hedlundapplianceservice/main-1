Diagnostic Scope

Dryer Failures We Repair

Dryer issues range from blown thermal cutoffs caused by restricted vent ducting to failed heating coils and worn drive components. We systematically test thermal and electrical circuits to pinpoint exact failures.

ISSUE 01

No Heat / Runs Cold

Testing heating elements, thermal fuses, high-limit thermostats, gas valve solenoids, and igniters.

ISSUE 02

Squeaking & Grinding Noises

Replacing worn drum support rollers, idler pulleys, front glide strips, and drive belt tensioners.

ISSUE 03

Drum Will Not Tumbler

Diagnosing snapped drive belts, seized drive motors, broken belt switches, and drum glides.

ISSUE 04

Long Dry Times

Evaluating internal blower wheels, airflow restrictions, cycling thermostats, and duct pressure loss.

ISSUE 05

Dryer Shuts Off Mid-Cycle

Testing motor thermal overload switches, main control board relays, and door switch contacts.

ISSUE 06

Won't Start / No Power

Checking incoming 240V power terminals, door latch switches, thermal cutoffs, and push-to-start switches.

Safety & Performance

Preventing Thermal Overheating & Fire Hazards

A dryer that runs hot without drying clothes often suffers from restricted air exhaust. When heat cannot escape, safety thermal fuses trip to protect your home from dangerous overheating.

Hedlund Appliance Service checks internal air channels, cleans internal lint traps, and ensures your dryer operates safely and efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jacksonville Dryer Repair FAQ

Why is my dryer turning on and tumbling, but producing zero heat?

On electric dryers, this is often caused by a burnt heating element or a tripped thermal fuse. On gas dryers, it typically stems from a failed igniter or weak gas valve solenoids.

Why does my dryer take two or three cycles to dry my clothes?

Long dry times are almost always caused by restricted lint airflow inside the internal blower housing or the home vent exhaust pipe.

Why is my dryer making a loud squeaking or thumping noise?

Squeaking usually indicates a worn idler pulley or dry drum support rollers. Thumping often points to a flat spot on a roller or a damaged belt.
