Diagnostic Scope

Washer Malfunctions We Repair

Modern washing machines rely on precise digital control boards, pressure switches, lid locks, and drive assemblies. Pinpoint accuracy during failure testing prevents unnecessary part replacements and gets laundry routine back on schedule fast.

ISSUE 01

Washer Not Draining

Inspection of drain pumps, filter blockages, pressure sensors, and control board signal lines causing standing water.

ISSUE 02

Spin Cycle Failures

Diagnosing tub drive belts, door/lid lock switches, motor stators, and drive couplings that stop spin action.

ISSUE 03

Active Water Leaks

Tracing leaking door boot seals, internal fill hoses, tub seal gaskets, and faulty water inlet valves.

ISSUE 04

Violent Shaking & Noise

Evaluating failed suspension rods, tub bearings, shock absorbers, and unaligned counterweights.

ISSUE 05

Agitation Problems

Testing wash plate splines, shift actuators, transmission gears, and wash motor assemblies.

ISSUE 06

Door & Lid Lock Errors

Resolving electronic latch failures, safety switch lockouts, and error codes preventing cycle startup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jacksonville Washer Repair FAQ

Why is my washer leaving my clothes soaking wet?

This is usually caused by a drain pump failure, a clogged pump filter, a stretched drive belt, or an unbalanced load trip during the final high-speed spin.

Why is my front-load washing machine leaking from the door?

Front-load door leaks are typically caused by torn or debris-covered door boot seals (gaskets), misaligned latches, or soap buildup in the door lip.

Is it worth replacing the bearings on a noisy washer?

It depends on the unit's age and tub structure. We evaluate tub bearing wear and structural integrity upfront to help determine if repair or replacement makes financial sense.
