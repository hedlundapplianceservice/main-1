Diagnostic Scope

Refrigerator Problems We Repair

A refrigerator problem can be anything from a simple airflow or defrost issue to a failing electrical or cooling component. Proper diagnosis matters because replacing parts without identifying the actual cause can waste time and money.

ISSUE 01

Refrigerator Not Cooling

Diagnosis of temperature problems, airflow restrictions, fans, defrost systems, controls and other causes of poor cooling.

ISSUE 02

Freezer Not Freezing

Inspection of airflow, frost buildup, evaporator systems, fans and temperature-control problems.

ISSUE 03

Ice Maker Problems

Troubleshooting ice makers that are not producing ice, overproducing, leaking or experiencing water-supply issues.

ISSUE 04

Water Leaks

Diagnosis of leaking water lines, drain problems, blocked defrost drains and other common refrigerator leaks.

ISSUE 05

Unusual Noises

Finding the source of clicking, buzzing, rattling, grinding or loud fan-related noises.

ISSUE 06

Defrost Problems

Diagnosis of excessive frost, blocked airflow and components that prevent the refrigerator from completing a proper defrost cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Jacksonville Refrigerator Repair FAQ

Why is my refrigerator running but not getting cold?

Several problems can cause this, including airflow issues, fan failures, defrost problems, electrical faults or cooling-system issues. Proper diagnosis is needed before determining the correct repair.

Can a refrigerator that is leaking water be repaired?

Many refrigerator leaks are repairable. Common causes include clogged drains, water-line problems and ice-maker or dispenser issues.

Why is my refrigerator making a loud noise?

Noises can come from fans, motors, compressors, ice makers or loose components. The sound and its location can help narrow down the source.

Should I repair or replace my refrigerator?

That depends on the appliance's age, condition, diagnosis and repair cost. An honest diagnosis helps determine whether repair is economically reasonable.
